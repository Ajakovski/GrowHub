The gap

Smart agriculture is split between cheap timers and expensive enterprise platforms

Home users get plastic boxes that water on a schedule. Farmers often rely on basic drip irrigation because commercial automation systems are too costly. GrowHub closes that gap with one modular product that works at both scales: practical, connected, and affordable.

Inside the appliance

Three floors, one continuous water loop

Every GrowHub is built on a fixed 40x70 cm footprint and runs full-scale Deep Water Culture (DWC). Water is pumped from the base reservoir up to the hydroponic grid and loops back down, so roots always sit in fresh, oxygenated, nutrient-rich water.

One product, every scale

From one kitchen to a commercial garden

We built GrowHub to close the gap between home growing and industrial agriculture. That's why it's modular: buy one to grow at home, or buy 40 and build a commercial garden. Same hardware, same dashboard, same AI plant care at every scale.

1

Buy one, grow at home

A single GrowHub is a premium 3-tier hydroponic appliance on a 40x70 cm footprint: an 18 L base reservoir, an 18-cup Deep Water Culture (DWC) grid and a smart LED canopy, monitored by a camera and sensors for reservoir level, water temperature and nutrients (EC).

  • Local AI detects plant stress before it is visible
  • Automatically manages water circulation, nutrients and light
  • Designed to look clean in kitchens and apartments
40

Buy 40, build a commercial garden

Daisy-chain GrowHub tiles into a full commercial installation. Every tile is the same 40x70 cm unit, so you can expand the garden one tile at a time as demand grows.

  • No re-engineering as you scale, just add tiles
  • Monitor every tile from one dashboard
  • Continuous loop DWC across the whole installation
Built to stack

Grow up, not out

Short on floor space? GrowHub units stack vertically on the same 40x70 cm footprint. Start with one and add more on top as your harvest grows, every level with its own DWC grid and smart LED canopy.

  • Triple the growing area without using more floor
  • Each level is a complete, self-contained GrowHub
  • Every unit shows up in the same dashboard
Tiling System

One Hub, as many tiles as you need

GrowHub scales with a Hub-and-Tile model. You daisy-chain complete 40x70 cm planter tiles to a central Hub, which supplies power and water to every tile attached to it.

The Hub

Central power and water

The Hub is the heart of the installation. It runs the continuous loop water circulation and the smart controllers, and powers every tile in the chain from a single connection point.

  • One power and water source for the whole chain
  • Every tile reports to one dashboard
The Tiles

40x70 cm floor tiles

Each planter tile shares the same fixed 40x70 cm footprint. Attach another tile to add a full 18-cup DWC grid under its own smart LED canopy, with water and power flowing through the chain.

  • Daisy-chain tiles as your growing needs expand
  • Same footprint and grid on every tile
