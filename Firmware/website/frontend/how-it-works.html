Step 1 · Sense

What the hardware measures

The Hub watches the shared reservoir. Each planter tile reports its own climate and flow. Together they give the AI a plant-level picture, not just a room thermometer.

Hub reservoir

Water level, water temperature, EC, and pH for the continuous DWC loop.

Every tile

Air temperature, humidity, light level, and water flow at that floor.

Optical node

Live camera frames so reviews can spot visual stress, not only numbers.

Step 2 · Review

AI plant stress, in plain language

On a schedule, GrowHub's AI service pulls the latest telemetry and camera context, then writes a status the dashboard can show: Healthy, Watch, or Action needed — plus a short summary a grower can read without opening a spreadsheet.

  • Runs locally against your hub API, not a mystery black box in the cloud UI
  • Status and summary land on the same dashboard partners already demo
  • You can still chat with the assistant about what the sensors are saying
Example status Watch

Light is low on tile 2 while water temperature sits near the top of the DWC comfort band. Worth checking canopy output and heater mode before leaves tip.

Step 3 · Actuate

Lights, pump, and heater follow policy

Controls aren't only toggles for a human. Each actuator can sit in auto, on, or off. In auto, care routines use the same control channel the dashboard exposes.

Lights

Canopy output for the grow cycle — auto when the AI or schedule should drive it.

Pump

Circulation through the continuous water loop across stacked or tiled floors.

Heater

Keeps reservoir temperature in the DWC band roots actually like.
