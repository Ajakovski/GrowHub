The gap

Home users get plastic boxes that water on a schedule. Farmers often rely on basic drip irrigation because commercial automation systems are too costly. GrowHub sits in the middle: practical, connected, and affordable.

One product, two solutions

The GrowHub Home Prototype

GrowHub home focuses on beatiful automated indoor growing. GrowHub Industrial focuses on affordable control for greenhouses and fields that already use drip irrigation.

01

Growhub Home

A modular indoor planter system sith one central hub, up to five planters a camera and sensors for light, temperature, humidity and nutrients.

  • Local AI detects plant stress before it is visible
  • Automatically adjusts water and light conditions
  • Designed to look clean in kitchens and apartments
02

GrowHub Industrial

A retrofit for existing drip irrigation: solar-powered pumps, ESP32 microcontrollers, sensor data, and automated fertilizer valves.

  • No expensive camera hardware at farm scale.
  • Remote control and mobile automation for farmers.
  • Custom per-hectare pricing for real deployments.
